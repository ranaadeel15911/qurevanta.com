## Who we are

**QureVanta Healthcare** is a medical billing and revenue cycle management company based in Winter Springs, Florida. We work with physician practices, specialty groups, and healthcare organizations that want their revenue cycle to be accurate, transparent, and dependable — so they can spend their time on patient care rather than paperwork.

Billing is the financial pulse of a practice. When claims are clean, denials are worked quickly, and payments are posted accurately, a practice has the stability to hire, grow, and invest in patient care. When those things go wrong, revenue leaks away quietly, one unpaid claim at a time. Our job is to stop those leaks.

## What we do

We support the full revenue cycle, from the moment a practice is set up to bill until the final payment is collected:

- **Setup and onboarding:** [medical credentialing](/services/medical-credentialing), [payer contracting](/services/payer-contract-negotiation), [practice management and EHR setup](/services/pm-ehr-setup-support), [ERA/EDI enrollment](/services/era-edi-setup), and [compliance support](/services/compliance-hipaa-support)

## How we work

Our work is organized into [specialized service teams](/teams) — billing and claims, credentialing and onboarding, denial and recovery, patient billing, and reporting and analytics. Each client has one accountable point of contact, supported by specialists who focus on their area every day.

We believe in a few simple principles:

- **Transparency.** You should always know where your revenue stands. We provide regular reports in plain language, and you keep full access to your own data.
- **Ownership.** Every claim and every task has an owner and a next step. Nothing is left waiting in a queue.
- **Prevention over rework.** Fixing the same denial again and again is expensive. We look for the root cause and fix the process upstream.
- **Respect for patients.** Patient billing is part of the patient experience. Statements should be clear, and conversations should be courteous.
- **Compliance and security.** We work under business associate agreements and follow HIPAA safeguards for the information entrusted to us.
