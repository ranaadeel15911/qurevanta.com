This Privacy Policy explains how **QureVanta Healthcare** ("QureVanta," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit **qurevanta.com** (the "Site") or contact us. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

When you submit a form on the Site — such as a request for a free audit or a contact message — we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

- Your name, work email address, and phone number
- Your practice or organization name and specialty
- The services you are interested in and any message you send

When you email or call us, we receive the information contained in your communication.

**Please do not submit protected health information (PHI) or patient details through the Site’s forms or by email.** Our website forms are intended only for business inquiries. Patient information is exchanged only after a client relationship and a business associate agreement are in place, through secure channels.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically collect technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring website, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the Site.

## How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to your inquiries and provide the information or services you request
- Prepare proposals, audits, and consultations you ask for
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site
- Display advertising on parts of the Site, as described below
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information.

## Where form submissions are stored

Form submissions may be stored using **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore)**, a service provided by Google LLC, and are accessible only to authorized QureVanta personnel. If form storage is unavailable, your browser may instead open your email application with your message pre-filled, so you can send it to us directly.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and the third-party services we use — analytics and advertising — set cookies or use similar technologies, as described below. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

## Analytics

We use **Google Analytics**, provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and how they arrived. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and pages viewed. We use this information in aggregate to improve the Site. You can learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and you can opt out by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Advertising and Google AdSense

We use **Google AdSense** to display advertisements on some pages of the Site, such as our articles.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.
- Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, visit [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through a consent message provided by Google.

## How we share information

We share information only:

- With service providers who help us operate the Site and our business, such as hosting (Vercel), form storage (Google Firebase), analytics (Google Analytics), and advertising (Google), under their own privacy terms
- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of QureVanta, our clients, or others
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy

## Data retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and for legitimate business purposes, such as maintaining records of client communications, and then delete or anonymize it.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have submitted through the Site by contacting us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws, and we will respond to requests as required by those laws.

## Children’s privacy

The Site is intended for healthcare businesses and professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Links to other websites

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
